## Research Overview

My research focuses on intelligent protection and fault analysis of modern power systems, particularly AC/DC, MVDC, HVDC, and converter-dominated networks. I am interested in developing fast, reliable, and robust protection methods for future power systems with high penetration of power electronic converters and inverter-based resources.

## Current Research Areas

### AI-Driven Power System Protection
Development of machine learning and deep learning methods for fault detection, classification, and location in AC, DC, and hybrid power networks.

### MVDC and HVDC Protection
Protection of medium-voltage and high-voltage DC networks, including fast fault identification and selective protection strategies.

### Modular Multilevel Converter Systems
Fault analysis and protection of MMC-based DC networks, with emphasis on converter fault behavior and short-time transient characteristics.

### Inverter-Based Resources
Protection challenges associated with renewable-energy-dominated and converter-interfaced power systems.

### Hybrid AC/DC Networks
Protection and fault diagnosis in interconnected AC/DC systems, including coordination between AC and DC protection schemes.
