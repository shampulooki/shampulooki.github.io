## Biography

I am a Postdoctoral Researcher at Seoul National University of Science and Technology (SeoulTech). My research focuses on power system protection, with particular emphasis on AC/DC, MVDC, and converter-dominated power systems.

My work focuses on developing AI-driven protection methods using machine learning and deep learning for fast and reliable fault detection, classification, and location. My current research interests include MMC-based MVDC networks, inverter-based resources, hybrid AC/DC systems, and intelligent protection of future power networks.

## Research Interests

- Power System Protection and Fault Analysis
- AC/DC and Hybrid AC/DC Power Networks
- MVDC and HVDC Protection
- Modular Multilevel Converter (MMC) Systems
- Artificial Intelligence for Power System Protection
- Machine Learning and Deep Learning
- Inverter-Based Resources and Renewable Energy Integration
- Fault Detection, Classification, and Location
